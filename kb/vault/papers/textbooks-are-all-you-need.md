## Claims

> [!note] CLAIM — training-recipes
> Textbook-quality synthetic data (Phi lineage) works, but for low-resource languages MT-translated data injects 'translationese' (non-native structures, NMT errors) that limits naturalness; prompt-space transforms recover up to +4.7pp on G-MMLU, and Phi-style 10-gram-overlap decontamination is needed to keep benchmarks clean.
>
> **Numbers:** Phi: 6B filtered + 1B synthetic textbook tokens; translationese mitigation +4.7pp G-MMLU; 10-gram decontam
> **Relevance:** Synthetic Kazakh is essential to pad the token budget, but must be generated by a Kazakh-fluent model (not raw MT) and prompt-space-diversified, then 10-gram decontaminated against KazMMLU/TUMLU/KazQAD to avoid inflating the very benchmarks the paper reports.
