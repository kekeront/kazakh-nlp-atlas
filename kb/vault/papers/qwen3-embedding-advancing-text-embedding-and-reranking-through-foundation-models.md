## Claims

> [!note] CLAIM — embed-sota
> As of the latest leaderboard snapshot (2026-05-17), Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B is the top open <=1B multilingual embedder on MTEB Multilingual (MMTEB), and the overall #1 is Tencent's KaLM-Embedding-Gemma3-12B-2511 — meaning the sub-1B crown has NOT moved since June 2025, so a new sub-600M model has a stable, beatable target.
>
> **Numbers:** Sub-1B: Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B 64.34 Mean(Task); multilingual-e5-large-instruct 63.22 (560M); EmbeddingGemma 61.15 (308M); bge-m3 59.56 (568M); jina-embeddings-v3 58.37 (570M). Overall: KaLM-Embedding-Gemma3-12B-2511 72.32 (11.76B, 3840d); Qwen3-Embedding-8B 70.58; Qwen3-4B 69.45; llama-embed-nemotron-8b 69.46.
> **Relevance:** Defines the exact score-to-beat table for the paper's embedding deliverable in the <=600M class.
> **Source:** https://www.codesota.com/benchmarks/mteb (snapshot 2026-05-17); https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.05176; https://developers.googleblog.com/en/introducing-embeddinggemma/ · **Sweep:** `embeddings-2026-07`

> [!note] CLAIM — embed-sota
> Recipe convergence across ALL 2025-2026 top small embedders (Qwen3-Embedding, EmbeddingGemma, KaLM-V2, Granite R2, Arctic 2.0): (1) multi-stage training — weakly-supervised contrastive pretraining on massive pairs, then supervised fine-tune with hard negatives; (2) InfoNCE with in-batch negatives and false-negative handling; (3) checkpoint merging/souping (slerp or averaging) as a free robustness gain; (4) MRL; (5) instruction-prefixed inputs; (6) distillation from a larger embedder/LLM teacher. No top model skips more than one of these.
>
> **Numbers:** 6/6 components in Qwen3 & Granite R2; EmbeddingGemma adds QAT; KaLM-V2 adds focal reweighting + online hard-negative mixing.
> **Relevance:** This is the de-facto standard checklist the paper's embedding training spec should implement (and reviewers will expect); deviations need justification.
> **Source:** arXiv 2506.05176, 2509.20354, 2506.20923, 2605.13521, 2412.04506 (cross-paper synthesis) · **Sweep:** `embeddings-2026-07`

> [!note] CLAIM — decoder-to-embedder
> Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B is the model to beat in the 0.6B class. It is initialized from Qwen3-0.6B-Base (same generative-backbone-to-embedder pattern we propose), uses last-token [EOS] pooling with instruction-aware queries, supports Matryoshka dims 32-1024 and 32K context, Apache 2.0. Its recipe: Stage 1 weak supervision on ~150M synthetic multilingual pairs generated by Qwen3-32B; filter to ~12M high-quality pairs (cosine >0.7) for supervised Stage 2; then slerp model merging across checkpoints.
>
> **Numbers:** MMTEB multilingual mean(task) 64.33; MTEB English v2 70.70; MTEB Code 75.41. No per-language Kazakh number is published anywhere — the on-Kazakh comparison is wide open.
> **Relevance:** Defines both the recipe template and the concrete target: beat 64.33-class models ON KAZAKH, where no number exists yet — we must build the benchmark that measures it.
> **Source:** arXiv 2506.05176 (Qwen3 Embedding technical report); https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B · **Sweep:** `embeddings-2026-07`

> [!note] CLAIM — embeddings-training
> Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B is the strongest sub-1B multilingual embedder to beat and the natural v1 backbone: it is a decoder (Qwen3-0.6B) converted to an instruction-aware embedder that KEEPS causal attention and uses last-token [EOS] pooling (not bidirectional/mean). MRL output 32-1024 dims, 32k context, >100 languages.
>
> **Numbers:** 0.6B params; emb dim 1024 (MRL 32-1024); 32k ctx; MTEB-Multilingual mean-task 64.33; MTEB-English v2 70.70; MTEB-Code 75.41; instruction format 'Instruct: {task}\nQuery:{q}'
> **Relevance:** TRANSFERABLE-UNTESTED (no Kazakh eval published). Since QymyzLM's v1 head sits on Qwen3-0.6B, this proves the exact backbone yields a top-tier 0.6B embedder with last-token+causal pooling — a drop-in template. Its 64.33 multilingual mean is the aspirational bar; but it is NOT evaluated on KazQAD/Kazakh.
> **Source:** arXiv:2506.05176 (Qwen3 Embedding Technical Report); HF Qwen/Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B card · **Sweep:** `slm-arch-for-kazakh`

> [!note] CLAIM — embeddings-training
> Qwen3-Embedding's full training recipe is reproducible on free compute at the 0.6B scale: 3 stages — (1) contrastive pre-train on ~150M synthetic weak-supervision pairs generated by Qwen3-32B across retrieval/bitext/classification/STS; (2) supervised fine-tune on ~7M labeled pairs (MS MARCO, NQ, HotpotQA...) + ~12M high-quality synthetic pairs FILTERED by cosine>0.7; (3) checkpoint model-merging via spherical linear interpolation (slerp).
>
> **Numbers:** ~150M synthetic weak pairs (Qwen3-32B generator); ~7M labeled + ~12M synthetic supervised pairs; cosine-sim filter >0.7; merge = slerp of fine-tune checkpoints
> **Relevance:** TRANSFERABLE-UNTESTED. Confirms the model-soup/weight-averaging trick (slerp of checkpoints) that also underpins Less-is-More's 0.5/0.5 base-average — a cheap, compute-free gain for qymyz-embed. Synthetic-pair generation via a strong Kazakh LLM (Qwen3-32B/Sherkala) is the scalable route past the ~61k real Kazakh pair ceiling.
> **Source:** arXiv:2506.05176 (html v1, Sec on training data) · **Sweep:** `slm-arch-for-kazakh`

> [!note] CLAIM — embeddings-training
> Pooling/attention verdict at <=1B is unsettled and must be an ablation in the design spec: Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B uses CAUSAL attention + last-token [EOS] pooling and reports it 'on par' with bidirectional+trainable pooling for most tasks; KaLM-V2 and LLM2Vec REMOVE the causal mask (bidirectional) + mean pooling and reach higher MTEB at the same 0.5B scale. Causal2Vec adds a lightweight bidirectional 'contextual token' prepended to a frozen-style decoder.
>
> **Numbers:** Causal2Vec on Sheared-LLaMA-1.3B: MTEB avg 62.63 (+0.24 from contextual token); KaLM-V2 (bidir+mean) 67.81 vs Qwen3-Emb-0.6B (causal+EOS) 64.33 multilingual — different data, not iso-controlled
> **Relevance:** TRANSFERABLE-UNTESTED. For the QymyzLM head, the two live options are (A) causal+EOS+instruction (cheapest, reuses generation weights unchanged) vs (B) mask-removal+mean-pooling (higher ceiling per KaLM but needs re-adaptation). Not iso-controlled across papers, so the spec should A/B both on KazQAD.
> **Source:** arXiv:2506.05176 (Qwen3-Emb); arXiv:2506.20923 (KaLM-V2); arXiv:2507.23386 (Causal2Vec) · **Sweep:** `slm-arch-for-kazakh`

> [!note] CLAIM — embeddings-training
> Matryoshka Representation Learning is now standard in every sub-1B SOTA embedder (Qwen3-Emb 32-1024, KaLM-V2 to 256-dim, gte-multilingual 128-768) and costs ~nothing at train time — a nested-dim loss term applied to contrastive + KL losses, preserving quality at truncated dims.
>
> **Numbers:** Qwen3-Emb MRL 32-1024; KaLM-V2 retains quality at 256-dim; gte-multilingual 128-768
> **Relevance:** TRANSFERABLE-UNTESTED. Free feature to ship in qymyz-embed v0/v1: enables 256-dim KazQAD retrieval indices (4x smaller than 1024) at minimal quality loss — matters for the on-device/modest-compute deployment story.

> [!note] CLAIM — embeddings-training
> Slerp/checkpoint model-merging and base-weight-averaging is a cross-cutting free-compute win confirmed independently: Qwen3-Embedding merges fine-tune checkpoints via slerp; Less-is-More (KB) 0.5/0.5 averages the fine-tune with the base mE5. Both realize gains with zero extra training.
>
> **Numbers:** Qwen3-Emb: slerp of multiple fine-tune checkpoints; Less-is-More: 0.5/0.5 weight-average with base mE5, ~10k pairs, lr 7e-5, batch 512, 5 epochs
> **Relevance:** TRANSFERABLE-UNTESTED on Kazakh beyond Less-is-More itself. Extends the KB's Less-is-More entry: two independent SOTA recipes rely on weight-space merging, so qymyz-embed v0 should average multiple KazQAD fine-tunes (seed/data variants) via slerp, not just base-averaging.
> **Source:** arXiv:2506.05176 (slerp); arXiv:2603.22290 (Less-is-More, KB) · **Sweep:** `slm-arch-for-kazakh`

> [!note] CLAIM — joint-generative-embedding-head-on-one-6
> [tested-on-Kazakh] The 'shared Qwen3-0.6B backbone -> embedder' path already has an existence proof AND a Kazakh-specific failure: Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B is initialized from Qwen3-0.6B-Base (our exact CPT base) and is the top open <=1B multilingual embedder on MMTEB (64.34 Mean(Task)) — but it is an embedding-only specialization (generation discarded, so it is a 'two separate models' datapoint, not joint), and on the only public Kazakh retrieval signal it loses badly to same-size encoders: BelebeleRetrieval kaz_Cyrl nDCG@10 0.7545 vs BGE-M3 0.9017 and mE5-large-instruct 0.8949; on Armenian (LRL proxy) it averages 47.38 vs mE5-base 64.30. Cause per Less-is-More: synthetic contrastive data skewed to high-resource languages.
>
> **Numbers:** MMTEB 64.34; Belebele kaz nDCG@10: Qwen3-Emb-0.6B 0.7545, BGE-M3 0.9017, mE5-large 0.8269, mE5-large-instruct 0.8949; Armenian avg: Qwen3-Emb-0.6B 47.38, EmbeddingGemma 52.43, mE5-base 64.30
> **Relevance:** The decoder-derived-embedder route underperforms XLM-R encoders on Kazakh-adjacent evidence at equal size — so the separate mE5 fine-tune remains the favored market vehicle, UNLESS Kazakh-CPT of the backbone (10B kk tokens) removes the data-skew cause; that removal is untested anywhere.
> **Source:** KB nodes: arXiv:2506.05176 (Qwen3 Embedding), arXiv:2603.22290 (Less-is-More), official mteb results repo BelebeleRetrieval.json (extracted 2026-07-03) — consolidated from KB, not re-verified · **Sweep:** `slm-arch-for-kazakh`
