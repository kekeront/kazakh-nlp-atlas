## Findings

> [!note] CLAIM — kazakh-morphological-segmentation-qualit
> KazMorphCorpus-2025 and every high-accuracy Kazakh segmentation result are measured on curated/gold/synthetic data, NOT noisy monolingual crawl. KazMorphCorpus-2025 is 150,000 sentences / 2.04M tokens from literature (EPUB), news, curated social media, Wikipedia and transcribed speech — hand-verified (~50K forms), Cohen's kappa 0.881. No published number exists for segmenter accuracy on CulturaX/HPLT/mC4-style text.
>
> **Numbers:** 150,000 sentences, 2,040,000 tokens; literature 45K / news 37.5K / social 30K / Wikipedia 22.5K / spoken 15K sentences; ~50,000 manually verified forms; POS kappa 0.881, lemma agreement 92.3%.
> **Relevance:** The whole novelty (morpheme-conditioned memory) and tokenizer edge are gated on segmentation quality at crawl scale — a number that literally does not exist in the literature. Producing it on your own CulturaX/HPLT shards would itself be a first-of-kind contribution and de-risk the claim.
