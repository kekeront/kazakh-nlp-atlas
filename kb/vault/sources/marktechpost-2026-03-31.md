## Findings

> [!note] CLAIM — sota-slm
> LFM2.5-350M (March 2026) pushes the token-to-parameter ratio to 80,000:1 by training a 350M model on 28T tokens plus scaled RL, running in under 1GB memory and beating models 2x its size on GPQA/MMLU-Pro/IFEval/GSM8K/MATH. Quality improves log-linearly from 350M to 24B on the same architecture.
>
> **Numbers:** 350M params, 28T tokens (80,000:1), <1GB RAM
> **Relevance:** The dominant lever at sub-1B in 2026 is extreme over-training (data quantity/quality), not parameter count. The Kazakh plan's ~9-10B tokens is ~3 orders of magnitude below this; closing it via cross-lingual/code/synthetic data is the single biggest risk-and-opportunity.
