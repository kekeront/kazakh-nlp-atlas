## Findings

> [!note] CLAIM — embed-kazakh
> issai/RAGBench_Kazakh (Apr 2026) is a machine-translated Kazakh port of RAGBench: 11,431 test examples in 12 domain subsets (TatQA 3,258, FinQA 2,144, PubMed/CUAD/customer-support etc.), question+documents+answer format, CC-BY-4.0 — the first kk RAG-grounding eval, but 100% MT with English-origin domains.
>
> **Numbers:** 11,431 examples; 12 subsets
> **Relevance:** Free end-to-end RAG eval for the combined SLM+embedder demo chapter; its MT nature is itself a citable bottleneck (no native kk RAG benchmark).
