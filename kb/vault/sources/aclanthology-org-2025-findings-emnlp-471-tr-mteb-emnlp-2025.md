## Findings

> [!note] CLAIM — decoder-to-embedder
> TR-MTEB (closest Turkic analogue) is a second warning: 26 Turkish datasets, 6 task types, a corpus of 34.2M weakly supervised Turkish pairs, contrastive pretraining + SFT — yet the Turkish-specialized models remained only 'competitive' and did NOT robustly surpass multilingual E5. Monolingual specialization with weak pairs alone does not win; pair quality and distillation matter more than pair count.
>
> **Numbers:** 26 datasets; 34.2M weak pairs; specialized models do not beat mE5 overall.
> **Relevance:** Calibrates our claim: 'beating all multilingual embedders on Kazakh' requires the AfriE5/KaLM distillation lever, not just scraped weak pairs.
