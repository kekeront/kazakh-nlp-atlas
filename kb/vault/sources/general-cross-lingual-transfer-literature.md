## Findings

> [!warning] UNCERTAIN — training-recipes
> Curriculum/order: starting from high-resource competence then adapting to the low-resource language exploits cross-lingual transfer and is effective, whereas naively mixing many languages from scratch triggers the 'curse of multilinguality' (per-language degradation as language count grows). For a focused set (Kazakh + Russian + Turkic + English) the curse is manageable.
>
> **Numbers:** curse-of-multilinguality worsens with #languages; high-resource-first curriculum aids transfer
> **Relevance:** Argues for a two-phase from-scratch curriculum: an English/Russian/Turkic-heavy early phase to build transferable structure, then a Kazakh-heavy stable+anneal phase — rather than a flat uniform-Kazakh mix or a many-language soup.
