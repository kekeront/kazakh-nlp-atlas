## Findings

> [!warning] UNCERTAIN — decoder-to-embedder
> Multilingual embedders underperform on Kazakh today: a 2025 comparative study on ~7,700 Kazakh question-context pairs (informatics domain) found LaBSE, multilingual-e5-small, GTE-multilingual-base, mMiniLM and Arctic-Embed v2.0 reach only Accuracy@1 of 0.59-0.63 vs 0.50 for TF-IDF — a thin margin over a lexical baseline.
>
> **Numbers:** Acc@1 0.59-0.63 vs TF-IDF 0.50.
> **Relevance:** Quantifies the gap a Kazakh-specialized embedder can exploit; also a reusable eval set.
