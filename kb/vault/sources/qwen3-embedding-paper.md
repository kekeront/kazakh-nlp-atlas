## Findings

> [!note] CLAIM — embed-sota
> Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B full spec: 28-layer decoder (Qwen3 base), last-token [EOS] pooling, 1024-dim output, 32K context, instruction-aware (instruction concatenated into query input). Training: (1) ~150M weakly-supervised synthetic pairs generated by Qwen3-32B across 4 task types (retrieval, bitext, classification, STS) with controlled query type/length/difficulty/language; (2) SFT on ~19M pairs (~7M labeled: MS MARCO/NQ/HotpotQA/MIRACL/MLDR/CodeSearchNet + ~12M synthetic filtered at cosine>0.7); (3) slerp model-merging of fine-tuning checkpoints. Loss: InfoNCE with in-batch negatives, K hard negatives, and false-negative masking (negative masked if its similarity exceeds positive-pair similarity by >0.1).
>
> **Numbers:** MTEB Multilingual: 64.33/69.45/70.58 for 0.6B/4B/8B; MTEB English v2: 70.70 for 0.6B. 150M weak pairs + 19M SFT pairs.
> **Relevance:** The reference recipe if the Kazakh embedder shares the decoder SLM backbone; every component (synthetic pairs from own LLM, cosine filtering, slerp merge, FN masking) is directly copyable at 10-100x smaller scale.
> **Source:** https://arxiv.org/html/2506.05176v1 (Qwen3 Embedding paper) · **Sweep:** `embeddings-2026-07`
